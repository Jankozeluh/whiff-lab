How the lab works

The data is Retrosheet's: every play of {{s.games_parsed|int}} AL and NL games, typed in by volunteers from scorebooks and newspapers, free for any use with its notice. The lab replays each game and checks that it ends on the official final score (all of them do). The models are built one step at a time, and each step is explained in a lab note in plain words, with what it gains over the last one on a test it never saw.

Everything reruns by itself once a year, after Retrosheet publishes the new season: free tools on a public repository, nothing to pay for and no account. The code and the tables are on GitHub.
